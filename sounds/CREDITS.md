# Credits

Banks 2-6 use samples from Sonic Pi (https://github.com/sonic-pi-net/sonic-pi, etc/samples),
dedicated to the public domain (CC0) by their authors on freesound.org, Arovane and The Black Dog.
They were processed (normalisation, trimming, tempo matching) by tools/build_banks.py.

The Gabber, Hardcore, Oldschool, Mainstream, New wave, Hardstyle and melody banks are fully synthesised by tools/gabber.py (no external samples).

The Anthem banks (sounds/anthem-*) were made with the free and open-source synthesizer Surge XT (https://surge-synthesizer.github.io), using its factory presets and community presets by Luna, Argitoth and others, and processed (mixing, mastering) outside this repository.

The Early hardcore bank (sounds/early-hardcore) is fully synthesised (no external samples) and was processed (distortion, reverb, levels) outside this repository.

The Anthem hooks and Lead riffs banks and the Basslines and Hardcore supersaw sounds (sounds/anthem-hooks, sounds/lead-riffs, sounds/basslines, sounds/hardcore-supersaw) were also made with Surge XT (the clean subs and 808s are synthesised directly), using its factory presets and community presets by Luna, Argitoth, Kinsey Dulcet, Rozzer, TNMG, Giana Brotherz and Bluelight, and processed (layering, echo, reverb, mastering) outside this repository.
